Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Mir Oboev Publisher (“the App”) is an internal tool of the wallpaper store «Мир обоев» (Mir Oboev), Dushanbe, Tajikistan (“we”, “us”). The App is used only by the store to publish its own promotional videos to the store’s own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

1. Information we access

When the store’s TikTok account authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, we receive only what TikTok provides for the scopes granted:

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm that videos go to the correct account.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to call the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Content we upload (video.upload, video.publish): videos and captions created by the store about its own products.

We do not access followers’ data, messages, contacts, location or any other user’s personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To upload and publish the store’s own videos to its TikTok account.
  • To check the publishing status of those videos.

We do not use the information for advertising profiles, analytics resale or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, as required to publish content.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a restricted environment and are never published in source code. Access is limited to the store’s owner and the automation that publishes content on their behalf.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use. You can revoke the App’s access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. After revocation the tokens stop working and are deleted from our storage. You may also ask us to delete any stored data using the contact details below.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children’s data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.
